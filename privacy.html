Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Powerless Utility collects information you submit, including your name, phone number, email, property location, electricity spending and solar preferences. We also record enquiry and consent details, including the submission time, page address, IP address and consent selection.

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, assess solar options, arrange consultations and maintain consent records. We may share relevant information with service providers that help manage enquiries and the solar provider preparing your requested proposal, or where required by law.

We retain information as needed to handle your enquiry and meet legal and recordkeeping obligations.

To ask about your information, request access, correction or deletion, or stop marketing contact, email info@powerlessutility.com. You may also reply STOP to marketing texts. Some records may need to be retained for legal purposes.

Policy updates will be posted here with a revised date.
