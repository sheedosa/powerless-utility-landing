FAQ

What does it cost me up front?

Nothing — $0 down financing is available for qualified homeowners.

Will I get bombarded with calls?

No. One call, from a real person, at the time you choose.

What if my roof isn’t right for solar?

We’ll tell you. About 1 in 5 homes we design turn out not to be a fit — you’ll get a straight answer either way.

Do panels work during an outage?

Grid-tied systems shut off for lineworker safety. With a battery, essential circuits stay on.
